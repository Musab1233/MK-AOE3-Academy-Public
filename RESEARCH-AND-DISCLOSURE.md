# Research & Public Disclosure

**Public policy summary · 2026-11-08**

## Purpose

The MK AOE3 Standards Institute & Professional Academy may conduct research across governed autonomy, accountable authority, execution evidence, assurance, and professional education.

Research visibility does not require disclosure of the underlying work. This public repository presents broad research themes and publication rules only.

## Public research themes

- **Authority and accountable autonomy:** the separation of technical capability, legitimate authority, specific authorization, and evidenced execution.
- **Evidence and execution truth:** provenance, independent observation, reconciliation, and the limits of signatures, hashes, and self-reported records.
- **Assurance and qualification:** explicit qualification states, responsible audit thinking, and prevention of unsupported claims.
- **Professional education and competency:** structured knowledge, synthetic teaching cases, demonstrated competence, and responsible assessment.

These are broad themes, not a register of active research candidates.

## What remains private

The following are not published in this repository:

- Live research-candidate titles, abstracts, working drafts, or full research text.
- Reviewer comments, private review records, research evidence, or working notes.
- Private research and disclosure registers.
- Unreleased publication plans or confidential collaboration material.
- Normative standards, examination material, learner records, Track A material, credentials, or owner-restricted records.

No live research candidate is disclosed by this policy.

## Controlled disclosure path

1. Working research remains private.
2. Candidate review remains private and controlled.
3. A separate public summary is prepared when publication is proposed.
4. The exact public summary receives a separate owner approval.
5. Only that approved artifact is published.
6. Published bytes are verified where practical, and the disclosure remains traceable in the private estate.

Approval for one summary, recipient, or channel does not authorize disclosure of the underlying research or any later version.

## Provenance without overclaiming

Private research controls may preserve original files, version history, recomputed SHA-256 hashes, review provenance, and disclosure decisions. These controls support integrity and traceability.

A hash, timestamp, copyright notice, repository record, or AI conversation does not independently prove authorship, invention, execution, occurrence, or legal ownership. Stronger legal evidence may require separate owner-approved legal or notarial measures.

## Public inquiries

Research, education, and collaboration inquiries may be prepared through the website’s local inquiry tool. The tool creates a draft on the visitor’s device and transmits nothing. Sending or sharing that draft is a separate decision.
